The rules, with their values

  • A trip starts for an Immediate or Urgent remote job, or when a community's oldest job has waited {{ multiple }} times its target.
  • {{ r.direct_tiers|join(', ') }} jobs go by the fastest route with no stops before them.
  • Otherwise, jobs on a route are added in need order, never quickest first, while the destination job still arrives within {{ '%.0f'|format((1 - r.anchor_slack_reserve) * 100) }}% of its remaining time to target (a job already past target: at most {{ r.overdue_delay_cap_hours }} h later than the fastest arrival), and no job on the trip is pushed past its target.
  • Route score = value of the jobs served on the way − {{ r.extra_hour_cost }} per extra hour of travel{% if r.charter_cost %} − {{ r.charter_cost }} for a charter flight{% endif %}. A job's value is {{ r.stop_value.Immediate }}, {{ r.stop_value.Urgent }} or {{ r.stop_value.Routine }} by tier, raised by up to {{ '%.0f'|format(r.wait_bonus_cap * 100) }}% for time already waited.
  • A trip holds {{ '%.0f'|format(t.capacity_hours_per_day * t.trip_days) }} hours of on-site work. Arrival times count {{ t.workday_hours }} crew hours a day. Restricted roads are driven at {{ '%.0f'|format(r.restricted_speed_factor * 100) }}% speed; closed roads are not used.
  • If a long trip would leave a later urgent job waiting past its target for the same crew, the long trip is shortened.

Road distances and speeds are approximate. None of this changes a job's tier, need score or rank.
